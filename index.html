How it works

  1. Pick a level. Beginner for gates and basic circuits, Advanced for breadboards and microcontrollers.
  2. Open a demo from the 🎓 Demos tab, or drag parts from 🧩 Parts onto the canvas.
  3. Wire it up. Drag from one pin or hole to another. Click switches and hold buttons.
  4. Experiment. Edit code, check truth tables, and save your circuits to a file.
